Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

rclone-gphotos is a personal, non-commercial tool used solely by its owner to copy photos and videos from the owner's own OneDrive account to the owner's own Google Photos account.

Data access

The application reads media files from the owner's OneDrive account and uploads them to the owner's Google Photos account. Files are streamed through the owner's own computer; they are not stored on, processed by, or transmitted to any third-party server.

Data sharing

No data is collected, sold, shared, or used for advertising. There is no analytics or tracking.

Data retention

The application stores only OAuth tokens on the owner's own computer to maintain access. No copies of media are retained after the migration.
